## Failure patterns the skill must address

1. **Silence becomes a rule** (1-4): an unexplained deletion becomes a prohibition.
2. **Scope creep on third-party instructions** (2-1): an attributed instruction is still applied beyond its stated scope.
3. **Flat confidence** (S1): one-example inferences sit beside explicit rules with no status.
4. **Rationale before result** (1-9).
5. **No durable format**: the records are prose that another agent or a later session can't merge or check.

The baseline is strong. The skill's job is narrow: fix these five failures without regressing the rest.
