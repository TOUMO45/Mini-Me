## Defects found by testing and fixed before release

1. **Recall missed relevant records** (S4, found before any agent run). One record that scored very high pushed three other "When writing a client proposal" records below the relative cutoff. **Fix:** a record is also kept when the brief covers at least half of its trigger condition, and recall fills up to *k* with positive matches. Regression test: `TestRecallS4.test_finds_all_relevant_rules`.
2. **Recall put another client's rule under "Apply"** (S3 v2 run). Kestrel's espresso palette was listed for Fjord. The agent caught it from the Scope, but the tool shouldn't depend on that. **Fix:** records whose trigger names a client, product, or project absent from the brief go to a separate "Check scope" group. Test: `test_other_client_record_is_not_in_apply`.
3. **Recall missed a rule over wording** (S3 terse brief). "Directions" vs "options", and no "client" in the brief. **Fix:** a small vocabulary map (option/direction/alternative, client/customer, deck/slides, …). Test asserts rule-002 is recalled from the terse brief.
