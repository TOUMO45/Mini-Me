## Defect found and fixed during this round

`explain` described a pending prediction as "Tentative: inferred from one example", which is the wording for a rule. It now says "a prediction only, until the user decides", with the track record. A regression test was added.

Benchmark (`eval/bench_scale.py`) unchanged: 6/6 relevant records and 2/2 traps at every size up to 4,010 records.
